# Lei Hackathon Run Of Show

## Timing: 3 minutes

### Slide 1 - The hook (30 seconds)

Every day, women navigate digital spaces that are fragmented, unsafe, or optimized for engagement instead of safety. Lèi, named after the Italian word for her, is a gated, trauma-informed ecosystem built for women to connect, discover, and grow safely.

Do not start with the feature list. Start with the tension: the platforms women use every day are not designed around their safety or real-world needs.

### Slide 2 - Education and career growth (45 seconds)

Lèi turns support into tangible progress. Discord-style hubs create focused spaces for Women in STEM, higher education, graduate research, and first-generation graduates. Campus SOS and student mutual aid help someone at Georgia State University find immediate supplies, study partners, or nearby peers. The Lèi AI Concierge adds career coaching, resume feedback, and grounding support in one private conversation.

Track connection: this is how a member moves from needing help to accessing knowledge, opportunity, and a next action.

### Slide 3 - Meaningful connections and physical safety (45 seconds)

Connection must translate into real life. The Teams-style wellbeing status lets a member answer, "What part of HER are you today?" without changing her content ranking. USA City Meetups help women find trusted local friendships in verified public settings. Safety beacons, legal rights resources, emergency hotlines, and support timers extend care beyond the screen.

Track connection: Lèi makes belonging contextual instead of generic.

### Slide 4 - Zero-trust architecture (30 seconds)

Trust is the product foundation. Credential uniqueness reduces burner accounts and duplicate signups. Production onboarding uses camera liveness checks and real six-digit SMS verification. Spatial access control gives members a primary sanctuary space and protects boundaries with an appeal workflow.

Be precise: the public demo uses a temporary developer code; real SMS is the production path through the server-side verification service.

### Slide 5 - Close (30 seconds)

Lèi is not just an app. It is infrastructure for women's autonomy, education, and safety. When women are safe, supported, and connected, they change the world.

End with the live demo URL and invite judges to watch one journey rather than every module.

## Questions to prepare for

- **Who is the first user?** Women students and early-career women who need immediate support and longer-term opportunity.
- **What is the track fit?** Education through campus and peer resources, career growth through coaching and opportunity sharing, and meaningful connection through safe contextual circles.
- **What is real today?** The interactive sanctuary, onboarding, spaces, wellbeing, Campus SOS, meetups, feed, and concierge prototype are live.
- **What comes next?** Pilot with one campus and one women-led career community, then measure access to resources, career actions, repeat participation, and resolved support requests.
- **How is safety handled?** Keep authentication and SMS server-side in production, use least-privilege access, moderate reports, and never use wellbeing status as a ranking signal.
