# Lèi Hackathon Run Of Show

## Core sentence

Lèi is a protected ecosystem that helps women turn a moment of need into a next step through education, career growth, and meaningful connection.

## Suggested timing: 4 minutes

### 0:00-0:30 - Open with the problem

Women do not lack advice or communities. They lack a trusted place that connects the right resource, person, and action at the moment they need it.

### 0:30-1:10 - Explain the solution

Lèi brings three outcomes into one protected experience: learn through campus and peer resources, grow through career guidance and coaching, and connect through contextual circles and meetups.

### 1:10-2:40 - Demo one journey

Use the developer demo URL:

`https://pkyeibrewu1.github.io/LEI/?developer=1`

1. Start at the blooming front gate.
2. Click **Get Started**.
3. Choose three states: **ambitious**, **overwhelmed**, and **connected**.
4. Continue to the sanctuary.
5. Show **Campus SOS** and explain the immediate peer-help use case.
6. Show **Wellbeing & Status** and update the member's state.
7. Show **US Meetups** or **Lèi AI Concierge** as the bridge from support to opportunity.
8. Use the developer verification code `274916` if the signup flow is needed.

Do not demo every tab. The story is the product.

### 2:40-3:20 - Explain trust

The gated entry, community covenant, contextual spaces, wellbeing controls, reporting, and SOS tools are not decoration. They are the trust layer that makes women willing to ask for help and return.

### 3:20-3:50 - Explain impact

We will measure time to first useful resource, career actions started, meaningful repeat participation, and resolved support requests. The north star is simple: every interaction should leave a member with a person, resource, or action that moves her forward.

### 3:50-4:00 - Close

Lèi is not another social feed. It is a protected place that helps women move from isolation to momentum.

## Judge questions to prepare for

- **Who is the first user?** Start with women students and early-career women who need both immediate support and longer-term opportunity.
- **What is technically real today?** The interactive sanctuary, onboarding flow, spaces, wellbeing, campus SOS, meetups, feed, and AI concierge prototype are implemented in the live demo.
- **What is next?** Pilot with one campus and one women-led career community, then measure whether members find help and take action faster.
- **How do you handle safety?** Keep authentication and SMS verification server-side in production, apply least-privilege access, moderate reports, and never treat wellbeing status as a ranking signal.
